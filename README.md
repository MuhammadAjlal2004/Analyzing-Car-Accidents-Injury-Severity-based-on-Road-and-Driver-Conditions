# Injury Severity following a Car Crash

by Team 2

**Categorical variables that may be used in analysis**

1.  Collision Type: This column provides a brief description of the collision.

    “Other”, “Single Vehicle”, “Straight Movement Angle”, “Same direction rear end”, “Head on left turn”, “Same direction sideswipe”, “Head on”, “Angle Meets Left Head on”

2.  Weather: Describes the weather when the collision occurred

     “Clear”, “Cloudy”, “Raining”, “N/A”, “Snow”

3.  Surface Condition: Describes the condition of the road where the collision occurred

    “Dry”, “Ice”, “Wet”, “N/A”

4.  Light: Describes the amount of light at the time of collision

     “Daylight”, “Dark Lights On”, “Dusk”, “Dawn”, “Dark no lights”

5.  Traffic Controls: Describes the presence of a traffic control

    “No controls”, “Stop Sign”, “Traffic signal”, “N/A”

6.  Driver Substance Abuse: Described whether or not the driver was under the influence of a substance or suspected to be under the influence of a substance

    “None detected”, “Unknown”, “Alcohol contributed”, “Alcohol Present”

7.  Injury Severity (Response Variable): Describes the severity of any injuries the driver endured from the collision 

    “No apparent injury”, “Suspected minor injury”, “Possible injury”, “Suspected serious injury”

8.  Driver Distraction: Describes if driver was distracted and what they were distracted by

    “Unknown”, “not distracted”, “Looked but did no see”, “talking or listening to cellular phone”, “Distracted by outside person, object, or event”, “eating or drinking”, “inattentive or lost in thought, “by moving object or vehicle”, “by other occupants”, “adjusting audio or climate control”, “other electronic device”

9.  Vehicle Damage: Describes the amount of damage the vehicle faced in the collision

     “superficial”, “unknown”, “no damage”, “disabling”, “functional”\

**Quantitative variables that may be used in analysis**

1.  Speed Limit (MPH): Speed limit (MPH) of the roadway where the collision occurred

2.  Vehicle Year: Year in which the vehicle involved in the collision was manufactured

3.  Crash Date: Date of collision of interest

**Additional Variables**

1.  Report Number: Incident report number assigned to the collision

2.  Local Case Number: Local case number assigned to the collision

3.  Agency Name: Name of agency that the crash was reported to

4.  ACRS Report Type: Brief description of how the collision was report (fatal accident, property damage, injury)

5.  Route Type: Type of route that the collision occurred on (Municipality, state, county)

6.  Road Name: Name of the road that the collision occurred on (if applicable)

7.  Cross-Street Name: Name of the cross street that the collision occurred on (if applicable)

8.  Off-road description: Brief description of where the collision occurred if not on a roadway (ex: parking lot entrance)

9.  Municipality: Name of municipality in which collision occurred

10. Related non-motorist: Description of other non-drivers involved in the collision (pedestrian, bicyclist, scooter, etc.)

11. Non-motorist substance abuse: Description of whether the non-motorist related to the collision was under the influence of a substance or suspected to be under the influence of a substance

12. Person ID: The unique identifier assigned by the government to the driver for identification and record-keeping purposes

13. Driver at fault:   A “yes” or “no” response to whether the driver was at fault for the collision

14. Circumstance: Brief description of the action that contributed to the collision

15. Drivers license state: State where the driver’s (of the collision vehicle) license was issued

16. Vehicle ID: The identification number of the vehicle involved in the collision

17. Vehicle first impact location: A description of where the vehicle was impacted in the collision using clockwise directions

18. Vehicle body type: Type of vehicle involved in the collision (passenger car, motorcycle, station wagon, pickup truck, etc.)

19. Vehicle movement: A brief description of the vehicle’s movement at the time of collision (speed, stopping or slowing, turning, etc.)

20. Vehicle going dir: a categorical variable of the direction the vehicle was travelling at time of collision (north, east, south, west)

21. Driverless vehicle:  A “yes” or “no” response to whether the vehicle was driverless at the time of collision

22. Parked vehicle: A “yes” or “no” response to whether the vehicle was parked at the time of collision

23. Vehicle make: the manufacturer of the vehicle in the collision

24. Vehicle model: the model of the vehicle in the collision

25. Latitude: latitude of vehicle at crash (numeric)

26. Longitude: longitude of vehicle at crash (numeric)

27. Location: a vector of latitude and longitude
